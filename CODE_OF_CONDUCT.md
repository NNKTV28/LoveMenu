# Code of Conduct

This project is small and run by one person. These rules apply to issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other place connected to it.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including with beginners.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what gets merged.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention
- Posting anyone's private information, including other players' names,
  messages or account details
- Asking for help with cheating that harms other players, stealing accounts,
  or getting paid content for free
- Spam, repeated off-topic posts, or bumping issues

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests,
and block users who break these rules. Serious cases can also be reported to
GitHub.

To report a problem privately, email **support@nikicoding.com**.
